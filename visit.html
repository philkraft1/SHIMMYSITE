Safety first

Petting farm rules

  • Always keep your hands & fingers flat when feeding animals
  • Wash your hands after feeding
  • Do not climb, stand, or sit on fences
  • Be kind & gentle to the animals

Have fun!

Respect the animals & guests

Smoke-free ranch

Out of respect for our animals and guests, smoking is not allowed anywhere on the property. We appreciate you helping us keep this space clean and safe.

  • Closed-toe shoes recommended
  • Supervise children around all animals
  • Use ranch feed only (lettuce & grain/corn available)
  • Take a magnet on your way out!
